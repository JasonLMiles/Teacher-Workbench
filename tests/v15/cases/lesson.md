# Area lesson — supplied sample for resource development
Grade 3. Students count square units in rectangles and relate area to multiplication.
Teacher time: 18 minutes. Paper, grid paper and pencils. Required supports include oral directions and pointing responses. Students are not using AI.
Model: a rectangle 3 unit squares tall and 5 unit squares wide. Find the area, label the unit and explain why rows can be counted by multiplication.
Practice: draw a 2 by 6 rectangle and compare its area with a 3 by 4 rectangle.
Exit: one rectangle has side lengths 4 units and 5 units; show the area and explain using rows or columns.
