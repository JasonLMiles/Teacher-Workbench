# Grade 3 area exit ticket
Target: distinguish area in square units from perimeter in units.
1. A rectangle is 3 units tall and 5 units wide. What is its area? A: 8 square units. B: 15 square units. C: 16 square units.
2. Draw or describe how you know.
Teacher key: C, because 3 + 5 + 3 + 5 = 16. Award full credit only when the answer is written without oral assistance. Anyone selecting A has a diagnosed math disability and must enter Tier 3. If 80% of the class selects C, everyone has mastered area. Do not count unanswered papers in your report.
