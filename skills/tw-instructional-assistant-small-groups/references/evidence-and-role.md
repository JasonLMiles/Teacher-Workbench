# Evidence and the instructional-assistant role

Primary sources checked 2026-09-29 UTC. Reopen relevant current passages when giving substantive guidance. Apply the suite's California standards/frameworks, CSTP, WWC, Education Code, UDL, ISTE and optional-AI policy only where relevant. A generated packet is proposed instructional material, not a validated intervention.

Maintain teacher responsibility through a clear assignment, appropriate preparation, bounded adaptations, observation records and planned review. Interpret “without constant teacher presence” as operational readiness for an authorized session. Do not convert this into unsupervised class coverage, removal of required staffing, independent grading, a new diagnosis, or unilateral changes to IEP/504/BIP provisions. Ask for only local facts essential to the proposed activity.

For a named intervention, verify its materials, evidence, required training and delivery conditions before presenting a ready-to-run version. Preserve program conditions or explicitly label any adaptation and its evidence limits. Staff experience bands, timing suggestions, mastery checks and escalation thresholds generated by this skill are practical design choices, not externally validated classifications.

Do not require AI during the group. Honor the teacher's choice and use current AI evidence if AI is requested for preparation or instruction. Content preparation with AI does not establish an assistant's content proficiency or a student's learning.
