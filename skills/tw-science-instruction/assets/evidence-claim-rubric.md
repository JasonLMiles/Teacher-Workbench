# Science evidence–claim rubric (proposed classroom rubric)
| Dimension | Strong evidence | Developing evidence | Evidence still needed |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Claim/model | Answers the question within evidence limits | Partly answers or overgeneralizes | Clear claim/model |
| Evidence | Selects relevant observations/data with units | Uses some relevant evidence with gaps | Specific observation/data |
| Reasoning | Connects evidence to scientific idea and alternatives | Connection partly explained | Explanation of connection |
| Revision/limits | Uses new evidence to revise; notes uncertainty | Acknowledges evidence but limited revision | Response to conflicting evidence |
Do not score English fluency here unless a separate language objective requires it.
