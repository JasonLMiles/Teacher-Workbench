# 35-minute student-work PLC
0–5: Name target and criteria; check task/support conditions.
5–12: Quietly describe three deidentified samples without ranking students.
12–20: Compare evidence with criteria; note disagreement and missing evidence.
20–28: Choose a reteach, practice, or extension move and a short follow-up task.
28–35: Assign preparation and evidence collection; set next review date.
Record decision, rationale, uncertainty and what would change the decision.
