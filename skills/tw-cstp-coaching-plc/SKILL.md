# Teacher Workbench Cstp Coaching Plc

## Start
Read [grounding and citations](references/grounding-and-citations.md) for every invocation. Read [coordination and capabilities](references/coordination.md) for the handoff and host-specific fallback rules. Use the [source directory](references/source-directory.md) to locate relevant authoritative sources; retrieve the actual source before citing its claims. Apply only the foundations relevant to the teacher's decision.

## Inputs
Required: teacher-selected practice question or growth goal. Optional: lesson, observation notes, student work, timeframe, collaboration norms and local evaluation context.

## Workflow
1. Clarify the teacher's desired student experience or learning and a narrow observable practice. Preserve teacher agency and separate developmental coaching from a requested formal evaluation.
2. Retrieve the relevant current CSTP standard/element, beginning with the 2024 official publication and checking for updates. Explain its connection to observable practice; do not confuse CSTP with student standards.
3. Co-design one change, evidence to collect, a baseline and a feasible review date. Use assets/coaching-cycle.md. Avoid causal claims from one informal observation.
4. Record low-inference notes: exact behavior, time, task, student response and counts where available. Keep interpretations in a separate field; never infer motivation or competence from a brief visit.
5. Facilitate the PLC protocol: describe work before interpreting; compare with shared criteria; consider alternative explanations and access; select one next move; revisit evidence. Share deidentified samples only.
6. Close with teacher reflection, action, a success signal and a decision rule. Provide a complete agenda or observation tool rather than a list of generic coaching questions.

## Deliver
Return a usable coaching cycle or PLC agenda, verified CSTP connection, observation form, evidence plan and follow-up. Label proposed success indicators as local inquiry criteria.

## Limits
Do not assign formal personnel ratings or claim a validated evaluation instrument unless the authorized instrument and process are provided. Coaching evidence is not a diagnosis of a teacher or student.

## Routing examples
Direct: “Help our PLC study student explanations.” Overlap: lesson redesign → planning/subject specialist. Boundary: unsupported personnel judgment → low-inference evidence and the applicable formal process.

## Rehearsal and material review
Use `tw-lesson-rehearsal` to practice a specific teaching move from an actual lesson, giving the teacher a turn before feedback. Treat simulated student responses as authored possibilities, not observation data. Use `tw-pedagogical-review` for a transparent review of actual instructional artifacts; its original operational criteria are not a validated teacher evaluation scale. Connect reflection to verified relevant CSTP elements and retain developmental teacher choice.
