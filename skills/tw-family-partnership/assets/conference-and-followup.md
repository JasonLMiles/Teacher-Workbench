# Reciprocal conference (20 minutes)
0–4: Family/student perspective and strengths.
4–9: Review concrete work with plain-language criteria.
9–14: Discuss possible explanations and needed access/support.
14–18: Agree on one feasible school action and optional family action.
18–20: Confirm understanding, language/channel preference and review date.
Record: family input; agreement; owner; date; evidence to review; unresolved question. Avoid unnecessary private information.
