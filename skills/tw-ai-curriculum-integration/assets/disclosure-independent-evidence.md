# Assistance disclosure and independent evidence
Student disclosure: “I used [tool/person/resource] for [specific purpose]. I checked [claims] with [sources]. I changed [part] because [reason]. My own contribution was [work].”
Follow local attribution/citation rules and specify permitted assistance before work begins.
Independent check: fresh problem/explanation under stated allowed-support conditions; score the content reasoning separately from the assisted product.
Never require revealing private chats or unrelated personal information. A detector score alone does not establish misconduct.
