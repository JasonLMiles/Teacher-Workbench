# Teacher Workbench California Edcode Policy

## Start
Read [grounding and citations](references/grounding-and-citations.md) for every invocation. Read [coordination and capabilities](references/coordination.md) for the handoff and host-specific fallback rules. Use the [source directory](references/source-directory.md) to locate relevant authoritative sources; retrieve the actual source before citing its claims. Apply only the foundations relevant to the teacher's decision.

## Inputs
Required: concrete action/question, school setting and relevant date. Resolve public/charter/private, grade/age, district policy or documented plan only when it changes applicability. Use deidentified facts.

## Workflow
1. Identify the actual issue and controlling facts before searching. Do not add a legal review to routine instruction where no legal issue changes the answer.
2. Retrieve the specific California Legislative Information code section and subdivision. Verify current text, effective and operative dates, amendments, exceptions and cross-references. Distinguish pending bills, chaptered laws, future operative provisions and superseded text.
3. Check Title 5 regulations, relevant federal authority, CDE/CTC guidance and supplied local policies separately. For federal or regulatory claims use the official legal text; search it at use. Do not label all these sources “Ed Code.”
4. Use assets/authority-register.md to connect each proposition to its authority and actual facts. Identify applicability conditions. A district rule may impose an additional local requirement but is not automatically state law.
5. Explain what is required, allowed, recommended, and unresolved. Use short accurate paraphrases with section/subdivision citations and links. If interpretation is disputed or consequential, identify the exact question for the district's responsible official or counsel; still complete the supported factual analysis.
6. Check the instructional recommendation against documented supports and actual policy without inventing eligibility, services, consent, disciplinary powers or approval.

## Deliver
Return a dated, fact-specific issue brief: governing authorities, implications, missing facts that affect the conclusion, practical next steps, and exact verified links. Separate legal text from educational judgment.

## Limits
Provide legal information, not a binding determination or representation. With no retrieval, do not assert current law from memory. Do not reproduce confidential student plans or adjudicate rights.

## Routing examples
Direct: “What current rules govern this assessment practice in our California public school?” Overlap: AI product adoption → edtech/AI plus the specific applicable law. Boundary: decide a disputed placement or legal case → identify authority and route the determination to authorized professionals.
