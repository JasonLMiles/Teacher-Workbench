## Verified WWC snapshot
At this review the [2014 guide](https://ies.ed.gov/ncee/WWC/PracticeGuide/19) reported:
- Recommendation 1: sustained, varied instruction in selected academic vocabulary — strong evidence.
- Recommendation 2: develop spoken and written English alongside subject learning — strong evidence.
- Recommendation 3: recurring supported practice in writing — minimal evidence.
- Recommendation 4: targeted small-group literacy/language support — moderate evidence.

Recheck the recommendation before citing it. Distinguish an evidence rating from a mandate, and an adapted classroom example from the practice as studied. Describe transfer limits for different ages, settings, or implementation conditions.
