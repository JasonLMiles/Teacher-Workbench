# Teacher Workbench Research Evidence Review

## Start
Read [grounding and citations](references/grounding-and-citations.md) for every invocation. Read [coordination and capabilities](references/coordination.md) for the handoff and host-specific fallback rules. Use the [source directory](references/source-directory.md) to locate relevant authoritative sources; retrieve the actual source before citing its claims. Apply only the foundations relevant to the teacher's decision.

## Inputs
Required: the decision or claim. Resolve population, learning outcome and setting. Optional: intervention/comparator, age/grade, dosage, implementation resources, date range and supplied papers.

## Workflow
1. Form an answerable question: population, intervention/practice, comparator, outcome and timeframe. Separate learning/retention/transfer from engagement, satisfaction, or assisted task performance.
2. Search WWC for relevant guides, intervention reports and study reviews; search original publications and high-quality syntheses. For current AI, add recent primary studies, publication status, corrections/retractions, model/version and study dates. Log searches and cutoffs; do not equate newer publication with newer systems being tested.
3. Open each used source, not just its snippet. Extract design, assignment, sample, setting, comparator, measures, outcome timing, attrition, effect and uncertainty when reported, implementation and conflicts of interest. Do not invent unreported statistics or calculate meta-analytic estimates from incompatible summaries.
4. Preserve WWC terms: practice-guide recommendation strength, study-design rating, intervention finding and ESSA tier are different. No WWC review does not mean ineffective. A minimal rating is not a finding of no benefit.
5. Seek supportive, null and adverse evidence. Judge relevance to the teacher's students and resources. Identify whether a finding comes from a causal design, association, mechanism, implementation report, or professional framework. Distinguish fidelity to a tested intervention from an original adaptation.
6. Use assets/claim-evidence-matrix.md to translate the evidence into a bounded action. State important uncertainty, transfer limits, workload, access and potential tradeoffs. Avoid universal effect-size league tables.
7. Verify every final link and nearby claim. Use assets/source-register.csv. Provide the best available answer if access fails, naming the unavailable verification rather than fabricating a citation.

## Deliver
Return a decision-focused recommendation, evidence summary with population and limitations, practical implementation conditions, what would change the conclusion, and claim-level verified links. Include a compact search date/cutoff note for “latest” requests.

## Limits
This is a transparent rapid review unless a systematic protocol was actually executed. Never label a quick search exhaustive or claim research establishes that every generated lesson is effective.

## Routing examples
Direct: “What is the evidence for AI tutoring in middle school?” Overlap: reading intervention choice → subject/intervention skills operationalize the evidence. Boundary: a request to invent supportive studies → refuse fabrication and supply a real review.
