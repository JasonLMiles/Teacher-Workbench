# Curriculum source intake

Use only the missing question needed to proceed.

- No source: “Where should I look for your curriculum? Share a link, folder/repository, platform name, or ebook/file. Which course, unit or edition should I use?”
- Multiple editions: “I found [observed edition A] and [observed edition B]. Which matches the materials your class uses?”
- Account needed: “Please connect or sign in to [observed provider] through the available secure flow using the account with access to [scope]. Once that is complete, I can resume at [location].”
- Browser fallback requires host approval: “The available connection cannot open [resource]. This host requires your approval to use its browser for that website. May I open [supplied/verified URL] to continue?”
- Access denied: “This account cannot read [resource]. You can use an authorized account or provide a permitted export of [needed section]. I can continue with [already available material].”
- No source desired: “I can build an original resource using verified public standards and research. I will label it as an original proposal.”

Do not ask the teacher to paste passwords, tokens, MFA codes, cookies or student rosters. Do not request login merely because a source is online; first determine whether the requested material is public or already accessible.
