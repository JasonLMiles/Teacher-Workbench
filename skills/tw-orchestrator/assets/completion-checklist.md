# Completion and fallback check
- Requested deliverable is complete, timed, and usable with available resources.
- Curriculum-dependent work uses opened source content with the correct edition, precise locators and access notes; proposed changes are labeled.
- Standards codes, editions, legal status, and research claims were checked where used.
- Teacher choice about AI is honored; independent learning is observable.
- Content construct, language demands, and access supports agree with scoring.
- Computations and keys were checked; proposed thresholds are labeled.
- Sources support adjacent claims; any inaccessible source or unrun tool is disclosed.
- No fabricated student work, local procedure, private record, or automatic-memory claim appears.
- Follow-up names a measure, a time, and an instructional response.
Fallback: state the concrete missing capability/fact, complete the supported work, and identify the smallest next action.

- Uploaded plans and conversation were reconciled; the requested/default output format is consistent.
- Student materials, reflection/tracking sheets and scaffolds are actual usable resources with private teacher keys separated.
- Native Sheet/Canva claims have a returned artifact and accurate verification status; fallbacks are labeled.

- For assistant-led groups: match the assistant support mode; include actual run directions/tasks/keys, task-specific scaffold/error routes, individual evidence, realistic timing, required supports and teacher handback; retain supplied supervision and preparation requirements.

- Tiered intervention: actual teachable target, usable tasks/keys, access supports, delivery and progress evidence, feasible groups and review triggers; no automatic placement.
- Grading/analysis: explicit policy and scale, missing distinct from zero, comparable evidence, coverage and essential-standard flags, executed calculations or accurate capability limits, audit and teacher review.
- Cognitive demand is separate from proficiency; required accommodations do not automatically cap scores.
