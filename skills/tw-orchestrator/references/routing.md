Retrieve and verify teacher-selected curriculum before curriculum-dependent design. Sequence goals/evidence before task design, task before supports/scoring review, and readiness before calibration. Resolve conflicting advice using applicable authority, evidence quality, population/setting, intended construct and constraints. Do not use a group product to infer each member's mastery or one composite to conceal disciplinary gaps.

## Intervention, taxonomy and grading

| Need | Lead | Conditional support |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tier 1/2/3 targeted activities with tasks, keys and scaffolds | tw-intervention-activities | Subject, access/language, progress monitoring; assistant packet when needed |
| Flexible intervention groups, staffing and schedule feasibility | tw-intervention-grouping | Activities, student work and progress; unknown evidence stays a diagnostic queue |
| Analyze or revise task cognitive demand using Bloom | tw-blooms-taxonomy | Assessment, subject and California alignment; distinguish knowledge/process dimensions |
| Marzano proficiency scales, score profiles or original rubrics | tw-marzano-grading-rubrics | Assessment and standards-based grading; preserve access supports |
| Standards-based grading policy, retakes and reporting | tw-standards-based-grading | Marzano scales, standards progress and local legal/policy questions |
| Dated standard evidence, time series, mastery transitions and domain rollups | tw-standards-progress-analysis | Standards-based grading; optional production for tables/charts |
| Matched pre/post growth, standardized change or paired intervals | tw-pre-post-growth-analysis | Assessment comparability and measurement; avoid causal claims |
| Weighted grades, retakes, drops, rounding, final-exam targets and what-if calculations | tw-grade-calculations | Stated grading policy and production; no live gradebook writes |
